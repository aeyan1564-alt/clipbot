Clipbot Autoposter - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

1. About Clipbot

Clipbot Autoposter is a private tool designed to assist with video editing, content review, scheduling and publishing to authorised social media accounts.

2. Information We Process

When an account is connected, Clipbot may process account identifiers, usernames, access tokens, video files, captions, publishing schedules and publishing results necessary to provide its functionality.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate connected accounts, prepare content, manage publishing workflows and communicate publishing results. Clipbot does not sell personal information.

4. Storage and Security

Clipbot uses server-side storage for operational data. Access credentials should be restricted to authorised operators. No access tokens or private account information are intentionally published on this website.

5. Third-Party Services

Clipbot may use TikTok, Instagram, Telegram, hosting infrastructure and AI services to carry out its functions. Information processed by these providers is subject to their respective policies.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Operational information is retained as needed for the service. Account owners may request disconnection and deletion of stored account-related data by contacting the operator.

7. Contact

For privacy enquiries or deletion requests, contact the Clipbot operator using the contact details provided through the service.
